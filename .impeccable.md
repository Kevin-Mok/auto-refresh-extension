## Design Context

### Users
People who revisit changing pages, including Twitch category pages, and need remembered intervals with independent tab controls.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, clear, dependable. Show the current page and timer without distraction.

### Aesthetic Direction
The approved prompt specifies a compact dark popup: charcoal surfaces, teal primary action, readable URLs, strong contrast and visible focus. Use bundled system fonts and no decorative animation.

### Design Principles
- Keep the timer, current page and tab status immediately visible.
- Distinguish saved page settings from this tab's state in plain language.
- Keep Stop, Resume and Clear accessible in the popup.
- Use text labels and keyboard focus; never rely on color alone.
