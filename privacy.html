Your pages stay on your device.

Auto Refresh Cache uses page URLs and your selected intervals to refresh the exact pages you choose. It has no publisher backend, analytics or advertising.

Privacy policy · Effective October 21, 2026 · Publisher: Kevin Mok

Data used for the refresh feature

The extension reads the active page URL when you open its popup. It receives browser navigation events and compares page URLs with saved exact-page rules to prevent stale reloads and resume matching pages. Timers and automatic refresh run only on sites you grant access to and exact pages you save. URLs can include query parameters. It does not inspect page text, form values, passwords or cookies, and it does not build or transmit a browsing-history log.

Storage and retention

Saved exact page URLs and refresh intervals are stored in Chrome’s extension-local storage until you clear them or remove the extension. They are not synchronized by this extension. Temporary tab identifiers, document identifiers, countdown deadlines, schedule tokens, status and per-tab pauses stay in Chrome’s extension session memory. Chrome clears that session state on browser restart, extension reload, update or disable. Timers may start again from saved rules.

Data sharing and network requests

The publisher does not receive, sell, share or use your page URLs or settings for advertising. The extension contains no analytics, remote executable code or service that uploads them.

An automatic refresh contacts the destination website in the same way a normal page reload does, including normal cookies and connection information such as your IP address. That website’s own data handling applies. Opening public help, privacy, source or support links contacts GitHub under GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages have no added analytics.

Permissions and your controls

Site access is requested from your Start click for the chosen scheme and host. Chrome grants that host access across its paths and ports, while refresh rules stay exact-page. The extension uses storage for settings/state, alarms for longer timers, scripting for bundled timer/reload guards, and navigation events to handle full-page and single-page-app navigation. It does not request Chrome’s history API or broad tabs permission.

Stop this tab pauses one tab; it does not delete its saved rule. Clear auto refresh deletes that URL’s saved rule and associated tab state. Manage site access separately in Chrome’s extension settings. Removing the extension deletes its local settings. You can also disable it to stop automatic refreshes.
